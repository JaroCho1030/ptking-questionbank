我要成為物治王

隱私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 25 日

中文English

「我要成為物治王」不收集、不儲存、也不分享你的任何個人資料。沒有帳號、沒有廣告，也沒有任何分析或追蹤工具。你的作答紀錄只存在你自己的裝置和你自己的 iCloud 帳號裡，開發者看不到。

1. 你的資料存在哪裡

  • 裝置上：作答紀錄、模擬考、錯題本、收藏、筆記與設定都存在你的裝置上。
  • iCloud 同步：如果你的裝置登入了 iCloud，這些資料會透過 Apple 的 iCloud（CloudKit 私人資料庫）同步到你自己的 iCloud 帳號，讓同一個 Apple 帳號的裝置看到相同紀錄。這份資料只有你能存取，開發者無法讀取。iCloud 的資料處理適用 Apple 隱私權政策。
  • 備份檔：App 會在「檔案」App 的「我的 iPhone › 物治王」資料夾自動備份；你也可以自選一個備份資料夾或手動匯出備份檔。這些檔案只存在你選的位置，由你自己管理。

2. 網路連線

App 唯一主動連上網路的時候，是向 GitHub 檢查與下載題庫更新（公開的題目檔案）。這個連線不會送出任何個人資料或作答紀錄；GitHub 和一般網站一樣可能記錄 IP 位址等連線資訊，適用 GitHub 隱私權聲明。

不連網也能使用全部功能。

3. 打賞（App 內購買）

「請作者喝咖啡」的付款由 Apple 的 App Store 處理，開發者拿不到你的姓名、Apple 帳號或付款資訊。App 只在你的裝置上記錄請過幾杯咖啡，用來顯示感謝文字。打賞不會解鎖任何功能。

4. 第三方

本 App 沒有使用任何廣告、分析、當機回報或追蹤 SDK，也不會和任何人分享或販售資料。

如果你在 iPhone 的「設定 › 隱私權與安全性 › 分析與改進」開啟「與 App 開發者分享」，Apple 可能提供不含個人身分的當機報告給開發者；這由 Apple 處理，你可以隨時在同一處關閉。

5. 刪除你的資料

  • 刪除 App 會移除裝置上的資料（「檔案」App 裡的自動備份也會一起刪除）。
  • 存在 iCloud 的資料：到 iPhone「設定 ›［你的名字］› iCloud › 管理儲存空間」刪除本 App 的資料。
  • 你自選資料夾裡的備份檔或匯出的檔案，直接刪除該檔案即可。

開發者手上沒有你的任何資料，所以也沒有需要向開發者申請刪除或匯出的資料。

6. 兒童隱私

本 App 不收集任何人的個人資料，包括 13 歲以下的兒童。

7. 政策變更

本政策若有變更，會更新這一頁並修改上方的生效日期。如果未來 App 改為收集任何資料，會先更新本頁與 App Store 上的隱私權標示。

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 25, 2026

“我要成為物治王” (PT King) does not collect, store, or share any of your personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, and no analytics or tracking tools. Your study records stay on your own devices and in your own iCloud account; the developer cannot see them.

1. Where your data is stored

  • On your device: answer history, mock exams, wrong-answer lists, bookmarks, notes, and settings are stored on your device.
  • iCloud sync: if your device is signed in to iCloud, this data is synced to your own iCloud account through Apple’s iCloud (CloudKit private database), so your devices using the same Apple Account show the same records. Only you can access this data; the developer cannot read it. iCloud is governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy.
  • Backup files: the app automatically saves backups to “On My iPhone › 物治王” in the Files app. You can also choose your own backup folder or export a backup file manually. These files exist only where you put them and are managed by you.

2. Network connections

The only time the app connects to the internet on its own is to check for and download question bank updates (public files) from GitHub. No personal data or study records are sent. Like any website, GitHub may log connection information such as IP addresses, as described in the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

All features work offline.

3. Tips (in-app purchases)

“Buy the developer a coffee” payments are handled by Apple’s App Store. The developer does not receive your name, Apple Account, or payment information. The app only keeps a count of coffees on your device to show a thank-you message. Tips do not unlock any features.

4. Third parties

The app contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, or tracking SDKs, and no data is shared with or sold to anyone.

If you turn on “Share With App Developers” in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements, Apple may provide the developer with crash reports that do not identify you. This is handled by Apple, and you can turn it off there at any time.

5. Deleting your data

  • Deleting the app removes its data from your device (including the automatic backups in the Files app).
  • Data in iCloud: go to Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Storage on your iPhone and delete this app’s data.
  • Backups in a folder you chose, or files you exported, can be deleted like any other file.

The developer holds none of your data, so there is nothing to request for deletion or export.

6. Children’s privacy

The app does not collect personal data from anyone, including children under 13.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, this page will be updated along with the effective date above. If the app ever starts collecting any data, this page and the App Store privacy label will be updated first.
