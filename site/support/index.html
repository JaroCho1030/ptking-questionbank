常見問題

需要網路嗎？

不需要。所有題目都在 App 裡，離線也能刷題、模擬考、看統計。有網路時 App 會自動檢查題庫更新。

要註冊帳號嗎？

不用。打開就能用，也不需要登入。

作答紀錄會同步到其他裝置嗎？

裝置登入 iCloud 就會自動同步到同一個 Apple 帳號的 iPhone、iPad。iCloud 空間已滿時會暫停同步，紀錄仍保留在裝置上；可以到「我的 › 設定」看同步狀態。

換手機或刪掉 App，紀錄會不見嗎？

有登入 iCloud 的話，在新裝置登入同一個 Apple 帳號、裝回 App，紀錄就會同步回來。沒有使用 iCloud 的話，建議到「我的 › 設定」選一個備份資料夾（例如 Google Drive 或隨身碟），之後可以用「從備份還原」救回紀錄。

題庫多久更新？

每年兩次國考公布試題後，以及考選部更正答案時。App 開啟時會自動檢查並下載，下次開啟 App 時換上新題庫，首頁會顯示更新內容。也可以到「我的 › 設定」手動檢查更新。

考選部更正答案後，我之前的作答怎麼算？

App 會依更正後的答案重新判定你以前的作答；統計、錯題本與模擬考分數都以更正後的答案為準。送分題不計分。

題目從哪裡來？

題目與標準答案取自考選部考畢試題查詢平臺的公開資料；主題分類由本 App 整理，若分類不合理也歡迎回報。

打賞會解鎖功能嗎？

不會。所有功能都免費，「請作者喝咖啡」純粹是支持作者，不影響任何功能。

FAQ

Do I need an internet connection?

No. All questions are built into the app, so practice, mock exams, and statistics work offline. When online, the app checks for question bank updates automatically.

Do I need an account?

No. Just open the app and start; there is no sign-in.

Will my records sync to my other devices?

Yes, if your devices are signed in to iCloud with the same Apple Account. If your iCloud storage is full, syncing pauses and your records stay on the device. You can see the sync status in “我的 (Me) › 設定 (Settings)”.

Will I lose my records if I switch phones or delete the app?

With iCloud, sign in with the same Apple Account on the new device and reinstall the app; your records will sync back. Without iCloud, choose a backup folder (e.g. Google Drive or a USB drive) in Settings, then use “Restore from Backup” to recover your records.

How often is the question bank updated?

After each national exam (twice a year) and whenever the Ministry of Examination corrects an answer. The app checks and downloads updates when opened and switches to the new bank the next time you open it; the home screen shows what changed. You can also check manually in Settings.

What happens to my past answers when an official answer is corrected?

The app re-grades your past answers using the corrected answer, and statistics, the wrong-answer list, and mock exam scores all follow it. Voided questions are not scored.

Where do the questions come from?

Questions and official answers come from public data on the Ministry of Examination’s past exam question platform. Topic classifications are made by this app; please let us know if any seem wrong.

Do tips unlock any features?

No. Every feature is free; tipping is simply a way to support the developer.
