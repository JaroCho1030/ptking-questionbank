常見問題

需要網路嗎？

不需要。所有題目都在 App 裡，離線也能刷題、模擬考、看統計。有網路時 App 會自動檢查題庫更新。只有題目留言板與讀書室需要網路。

要註冊帳號嗎？

不用。打開就能用，也不需要登入。留言板與讀書室用裝置上的 iCloud 辨認同一個人，不用另外註冊；沒有登入 iCloud 可以看留言，但不能留言，也不能使用讀書室。

作答紀錄會同步到其他裝置嗎？

裝置登入 iCloud 就會自動同步到同一個 Apple 帳號的 iPhone、iPad。iCloud 空間已滿時會暫停同步，紀錄仍保留在裝置上；可以到「我的 › 設定」看同步狀態。

換手機或刪掉 App，紀錄會不見嗎？

有登入 iCloud 的話，在新裝置登入同一個 Apple 帳號、裝回 App，紀錄就會同步回來。沒有使用 iCloud 的話，建議到「我的 › 設定」選一個備份資料夾（例如 Google Drive 或隨身碟），之後可以用「從備份還原」救回紀錄。

題庫多久更新？

每年兩次國考公布試題後，以及考選部更正答案時。App 開啟時會自動檢查並下載，下次開啟 App 時換上新題庫，首頁會顯示更新內容。也可以到「我的 › 設定」手動檢查更新。

考選部更正答案後，我之前的作答怎麼算？

App 會依更正後的答案重新判定你以前的作答；統計、錯題本與模擬考分數都以更正後的答案為準。送分題不計分。

題目從哪裡來？

題目與標準答案取自考選部考畢試題查詢平臺的公開資料；主題分類由本 App 整理，若分類不合理也歡迎回報。

打賞會解鎖功能嗎？

不會。所有功能都免費，「請作者喝咖啡」純粹是支持作者，不影響任何功能。

留言規範

每一題都有公開的留言板，用來補充解析、討論爭議題、分享記法。留言前需要同意以下規範（第一次開 App 時，或第一次留言前同意；同意後不能撤回，規範全文隨時可在「我的 › 設定 › 留言」查看）。

留言會顯示頭像與「暱稱 #4F2A」：暱稱自己取、頭像從內建圖案選（不能上傳照片），都在「我的」最上方改；暱稱 7 天能改一次、頭像隨時能換，改了以後以前的留言也會一起換；後面 4 碼由系統產生、不能改，同一個 Apple 帳號在每台裝置都一樣。

不可以張貼

  • 廣告、洗版、推銷或導流（例如「加 LINE」、代辦、博弈）。
  • 人身攻擊、騷擾、歧視、仇恨言論或威脅。
  • 任何人的個人資料，包括你自己的（真實姓名、電話、email、地址、社群帳號等）。
  • 色情、暴力或其他違法內容。
  • 故意張貼錯誤答案或誤導他人。討論中說錯不算違規，看到錯誤請用回覆指正。

違規怎麼處理

  • 送出前會過濾不適當字詞（暱稱也是）。
  • 每則留言都可以檢舉；被 3 位不同使用者檢舉的留言會自動隱藏，等待人工處理。
  • 開發者每天查看檢舉，會移除違規留言；情節重大或屢次違規者會被停止使用社群功能（留言對所有人隱藏、不能再留言，也不能使用讀書室）。
  • 你也可以封鎖任何使用者，封鎖後就看不到他的留言，可在「我的 › 設定 › 留言」解除。

免責聲明

留言是使用者的個人意見，不代表本 App 或考選部的立場，也不是醫療建議。正確答案以考選部公告為準；考選部更正答案後，更正前的留言會標示「留言時正解為 X」。

刪除與申訴

你可以隨時刪除自己的留言（留言不能編輯）。想一次刪除全部留言，或對處理結果有疑問，請來信 bunbitmail@gmail.com，附上你的「暱稱 #後綴」與相關題號。資料怎麼處理請看隱私政策第 3 節。

讀書室

讀書室是幾個熟人一起刷題的小房間：看得到彼此的頭像、暱稱和狀態簽名，可以「敲一下」提醒對方該刷題了。讀書室不顯示任何讀書數據或上線狀態（題數、連續天數、是不是正在讀書都不會出現），不用跟誰比。

怎麼用

  • 從首頁進「讀書室」，建立一間（房名 1–12 字），再把邀請連結用 LINE、訊息等傳給朋友；朋友點連結就能加入。沒有好友搜尋，只能用邀請連結進房。
  • 一間最多 8 人（含房主）；一個人同時只能在一間，要加入別間得先離開目前的讀書室。
  • 點自己可以改狀態簽名（最多 30 字，留空就不顯示）；點房友可以敲一下，三句固定句子選一句，對方會收到推播。同一位房友 3 小時內只能敲一次。
  • 不想收到敲一下：到「我的 › 設定 › 讀書室」關掉「接收敲一下」（還是能敲別人）。
  • 房主可以改房名、關閉或重新開放邀請連結、移出成員、解散讀書室；成員可以隨時離開。
  • 需要網路，也要登入 iCloud；暱稱、頭像與規範同意跟留言板共用，沒設定過的話第一次進房前會請你設定。

規範

  • 房名和狀態簽名適用留言規範，送出前會過濾不適當字詞。
  • 房友的「⋯」可以靜音（收不到他的敲一下、看不到他的狀態簽名，可在「我的 › 設定 › 讀書室 › 靜音名單」解除）或檢舉（寄信給開發者）；房主還可以把他移出讀書室。
  • 房間內容存在房主的 iCloud，開發者讀不到，所以沒辦法替你刪除房裡的內容；違規情節嚴重的使用者會被停止使用社群功能（留言板與讀書室一起）。

資料怎麼處理請看隱私政策第 4 節。

FAQ

Do I need an internet connection?

No. All questions are built into the app, so practice, mock exams, and statistics work offline. When online, the app checks for question bank updates automatically. Only the question comment board and study rooms need a connection.

Do I need an account?

No. Just open the app and start; there is no sign-in. The comment board and study rooms recognize you through the iCloud account on your device, so no separate registration is needed; without iCloud you can read comments but cannot post or use study rooms.

Will my records sync to my other devices?

Yes, if your devices are signed in to iCloud with the same Apple Account. If your iCloud storage is full, syncing pauses and your records stay on the device. You can see the sync status in “我的 (Me) › 設定 (Settings)”.

Will I lose my records if I switch phones or delete the app?

With iCloud, sign in with the same Apple Account on the new device and reinstall the app; your records will sync back. Without iCloud, choose a backup folder (e.g. Google Drive or a USB drive) in Settings, then use “Restore from Backup” to recover your records.

How often is the question bank updated?

After each national exam (twice a year) and whenever the Ministry of Examination corrects an answer. The app checks and downloads updates when opened and switches to the new bank the next time you open it; the home screen shows what changed. You can also check manually in Settings.

What happens to my past answers when an official answer is corrected?

The app re-grades your past answers using the corrected answer, and statistics, the wrong-answer list, and mock exam scores all follow it. Voided questions are not scored.

Where do the questions come from?

Questions and official answers come from public data on the Ministry of Examination’s past exam question platform. Topic classifications are made by this app; please let us know if any seem wrong.

Do tips unlock any features?

No. Every feature is free; tipping is simply a way to support the developer.

Comment rules

Each question has a public comment board for adding explanations, discussing disputed questions, and sharing memory tricks. You must agree to these rules before posting (when you first open the app or before your first comment). Once given, agreement cannot be withdrawn; you can read the rules any time in 我的 (Me) › 設定 (Settings) › 留言 (Comments).

Comments show an avatar and “nickname #4F2A”. You choose the nickname and pick an avatar from built-in icons (photos cannot be uploaded), both at the top of 我的 (Me); the nickname can change once every 7 days and the avatar any time, and your earlier comments change too. The 4-character suffix is generated by the system, cannot be changed, and is the same on all devices using the same Apple Account.

Do not post

  • Ads, spam, self-promotion, or links that steer people elsewhere (e.g. “add me on LINE”, paid services, gambling).
  • Personal attacks, harassment, discrimination, hate speech, or threats.
  • Anyone’s personal information, including your own (real name, phone, email, address, social media accounts, etc.).
  • Sexual, violent, or otherwise illegal content.
  • Deliberately wrong answers or content meant to mislead. Honest mistakes in discussion are not a violation; if you spot one, reply with a correction.

How violations are handled

  • Comments and nicknames are checked against a word filter before posting.
  • Every comment can be reported; a comment reported by 3 different users is hidden automatically until it is reviewed.
  • The developer reviews reports daily and removes comments that break these rules; serious or repeated violators are suspended from community features (their comments are hidden from everyone, they can no longer post, and they cannot use study rooms).
  • You can also block any user to stop seeing their comments; unblock them in 我的 (Me) › 設定 (Settings) › 留言 (Comments).

Disclaimer

Comments are users’ personal opinions. They do not represent the views of this app or the Ministry of Examination and are not medical advice. The official answers published by the Ministry of Examination prevail; after an official correction, earlier comments are labeled with the answer at the time they were posted.

Deletion and appeals

You can delete your own comments at any time (comments cannot be edited). To delete all your comments at once, or if you have questions about a decision, email bunbitmail@gmail.com with your “nickname #suffix” and the related question numbers. Study rooms

A study room is a small room for friends who practice together: members see each other’s avatar, nickname, and status line, and can “nudge” each other to study. Study rooms show no study statistics or online status (no question counts, streaks, or whether someone is studying), so there is nothing to compare.

How it works

  • Open 讀書室 (Study Room) from the home screen, create a room (name 1–12 characters), and send the invite link to friends via LINE, Messages, etc.; they join by tapping the link. There is no friend search; invite links are the only way in.
  • A room holds up to 8 people (including the owner). You can be in only one room at a time; to join another, leave your current room first.
  • Tap yourself to change your status line (max 30 characters; leave it empty to hide it). Tap a roommate to nudge them with one of three fixed phrases; they get a push notification. You can nudge the same person once every 3 hours.
  • To stop receiving nudges, turn off “接收敲一下 (Receive nudges)” in 我的 (Me) › 設定 (Settings) › 讀書室 (Study Room). You can still nudge others.
  • The owner can rename the room, turn the invite link off or on, remove members, and dissolve the room; members can leave at any time.
  • Study rooms need an internet connection and iCloud. Your nickname, avatar, and agreement to the rules are shared with the comment board; if you haven’t set them up, you’ll be asked before entering a room.

Rules

  • Room names and status lines follow the comment rules and are checked against a word filter.
  • From a roommate’s “⋯” menu you can mute them (no nudges from them and their status line is hidden; undo in 我的 (Me) › 設定 (Settings) › 讀書室 (Study Room) › 靜音名單 (Muted)) or report them (by email to the developer); the owner can also remove them from the room.
  • Room contents are stored in the owner’s iCloud and cannot be read by the developer, so the developer cannot delete anything inside a room for you; users with serious violations are suspended from community features (the comment board and study rooms together).

See section 4 of the Privacy Policy for how this data is handled.
